# Polish gold cases: conventions, edits, and unreachable cases

Every Polish case is a sentence written for Verbalis (`"source": "verbalis"`); NeMo and WeTextProcessing have no
Polish. A case lists every reading a native speaker accepts; the conventions behind those lists:

- **Agreement:** numbers take the case of a preposition or the noun after them (`z 5 kolegami` → "z pięcioma
  kolegami"), feminine `dwie` (`2 kobiety`), and the masculine personal form before persons (`15 pracowników` →
  "piętnastu pracowników").
- **Decimals:** "przecinek" with the fraction as a number (`3,14` → "trzy przecinek czternaście"), digit by digit from
  five decimal digits; `,5` may also read "i pół". The noun after a decimal is in the genitive singular.
- **Ranges:** the hyphen reading (`10-15 osób` → "dziesięć-piętnaście osób") and "do" with the end in the genitive
  ("dziesięć do piętnastu osób", "od dziesięciu do piętnastu osób") are all accepted. After a noun of amount, the
  genitive of both ends is accepted too (`Temperatura 20-25°C` → "temperatura dwudziestu-dwudziestu pięciu stopni").
- **Phones:** groups of three as numbers, groups of two as numbers, a leading zero read as "zero".
- **Fractions:** ordinal denominators (`2/3` → "dwie trzecie"), `1/2` before a noun also "pół".
