# French gold set: edited readings and unreachable cases

## Readings added (equally correct conventions)

- `les 80s`: added "les années quatre-vingt". "les eighties" is an anglicism; "les années quatre-vingt" is the standard
  French reading of the decade.
- `24,1313`: added "vingt-quatre virgule un trois un trois". Four decimals or more are read digit by digit, as people
  read long decimals; reading them in pairs ("treize treize") is another convention.
- `2/9`: added "deux sur neuf". In running text, a bare fraction with a numerator above 1 is mostly a score or a part
  of a series (5/9 au tir, épisode 2/9), read with "sur"; Verbalis reads it as a fraction ("deux neuvièmes") when a
  word marks it as one (2/9 de la surface).
- `6°`, `10°`: added "six degrés", "dix degrés". In French text the degree sign after a number is degrees (il fait
  6°); the ordinal use (6° régiment) is rare, and "1°, 2°" in legal enumerations reads "premièrement".
- `1 000 000 000e`: added "milliardième". The ordinal of a scale word drops "un", as "millième" and "millionième" do;
  "un milliardième" also reads the fraction 1/1 000 000 000.

## Readings corrected

- `Mˡˡᵉˢ`: "mademoiselles" replaced with "mesdemoiselles", the plural of "mademoiselle".

## Unreachable cases

None.
